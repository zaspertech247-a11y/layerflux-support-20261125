Follow heat through a panel

LayerFlux is an iPhone utility for comparing the insulation and recurring heat-flow paths in a wall or enclosure panel.

Start with a cross-section

Add the layers that cover the whole panel and the stacks for individual paths. Inspect how each path contributes, compare a second construction under the same inputs, and freeze a report for PDF or CSV export.
