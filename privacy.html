LayerFlux Privacy

Your workspace

LayerFlux does not require an account and uses no advertising or analytics SDK. Assembly names, measurements and frozen reports are stored in the app's workspace on your device. The thermal calculation runs on your device.

Exporting and deleting

When you use Share PDF or Share CSV, the system share sheet lets you choose where the file goes. Use the project's or report's delete action to remove individual saved entries. Reports → About → Clear local workspace removes the app's saved projects and reports after confirmation. Copies shared elsewhere remain in the locations you chose.
