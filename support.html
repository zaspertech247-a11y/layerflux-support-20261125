LayerFlux Support

Compare two insulation stacks at the same boundary temperatures and save the results.

Open the Workshop panel example

  1. In Assemblies, tap Load example, then Open cross-section. The panel is 12 m². Side A is 20 °C and Side B is 5 °C. The baseline estimates 84.64 W from A to B.
  2. Use the path selector to choose Framing strip. This route covers 15% of panel area and accounts for 30.00 W in the model. The temperature immediately after the A-side common layer is 15.00 °C.
  3. Tap Compare variants, then Duplicate baseline. In the alternative, add a Direct R layer of 0.60 m²·K/W to the Side B shared stack. Save the layer and the assembly. The same panel now estimates 63.00 W in the alternative.
  4. Tap Freeze comparison. In Report detail use Preview PDF, Share PDF or Share CSV. The report stays in Reports if you edit the source later.

Understand the scope

The model describes steady conduction along independent paths based on entered values. Differences in geometry, air leakage, moisture, thermal mass and changing conditions may change measured results. LayerFlux is for estimating and comparing scenarios with your own inputs.
